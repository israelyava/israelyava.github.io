# About Me

I am an academic and researcher in the field of **Electrical Engineering and Telecommunications**, with research interests spanning **artificial intelligence, machine learning, signal processing, telecommunications, wireless communications, and information technologies**.

My academic and research activities have focused on the development and application of computational and intelligent methods to address problems in engineering, communications, and related technological fields. I am also involved in **teaching, research, and the supervision of undergraduate and graduate theses**, supporting students in the development of research projects in engineering and technology.
